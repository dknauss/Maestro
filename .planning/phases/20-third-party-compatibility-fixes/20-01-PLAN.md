<objective>
Lay the pure, WP-light foundation for COMPAT-04 level-qualified match keys:
a testable helper that parses a `parent>child` submenu key and normalizes each
half independently, plus `Config::sanitize()` support that accepts qualified
submenu keys and drops icons on them. No replay-engine wiring in this plan —
that lands in 20-02 against this foundation.

Purpose: A top-level slug and a same-slug submenu must become distinct override
keys (`edit.php?post_type=product` vs `edit.php?post_type=product>edit.php?post_type=product`).
This plan builds the storage/normalization contract both replay and the editor
model will consume.
Output: A pure qualified-key helper (unit-tested) and a sanitize() that round-trips
qualified keys while enforcing the "no icon on submenu" rule.
</objective>
